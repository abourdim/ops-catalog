📚 How It Works
  1. In Raft, nodes start as Followers. When a Follower times out waiting for a heartbeat, it becomes a Candidate and requests votes.
  2. A Candidate wins the election by receiving votes from a majority of nodes. It then becomes the Leader for that term.
  3. The Leader sends periodic heartbeats to all Followers and replicates log entries to maintain consistency.
  4. If the Leader crashes, Followers detect the missing heartbeats and trigger a new election, incrementing the term number.

❓ Help
What is Raft consensus?

Raft is a distributed consensus algorithm that elects a leader to manage log replication across a cluster of nodes.

What is a term?

A term is a logical clock in Raft. Each election increments the term number, ensuring consistency across leadership changes.

What is split brain?

Split brain occurs when a network partition divides the cluster, potentially causing two partitions to operate independently.

How does log replication work?

The leader appends entries to its log and sends them to followers. Once a majority acknowledges, the entry is committed.

🗳️ Raft Algorithm

Raft decomposes consensus into leader election, log replication, and safety. It guarantees that only one leader exists per term.

🛡️ PBFT

Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance handles malicious nodes. It requires 3f+1 nodes to tolerate f faulty nodes.

💓 Heartbeats

Leaders send periodic heartbeats to maintain authority. Missing heartbeats trigger new elections.

📋 Log Commit

An entry is committed once replicated to a majority. Committed entries are durable and applied to the state machine.
