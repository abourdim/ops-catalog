# How-To Guide — Channel Heatmap

## Overview
Visualizes WiFi channel congestion across all 14 channels in the 2.4 GHz band.

## Steps
1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Click **Scan** to start channel analysis
3. Observe the heatmap: green = low, yellow = medium, red = high congestion
4. Click any channel cell to see detailed AP information
5. Check **Signal Strength Chart** for a bar chart comparison
6. View **AP List by Channel** for complete listings

## Tips
- Channels 1, 6, and 11 are the only non-overlapping 2.4 GHz channels
- New APs are discovered over time during the scan
- The freest channel is recommended for your own AP placement
