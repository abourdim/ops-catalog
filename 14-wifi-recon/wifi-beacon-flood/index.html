🎓 How It Works

A beacon flood attack creates hundreds of fake access points by broadcasting forged beacon frames on multiple channels. This overwhelms WiFi scanners and confuses users trying to find legitimate networks. Some fake APs use hidden SSIDs. This simulation shows how quickly the airwaves can be polluted, making it nearly impossible to distinguish real APs from fake ones.

❓ Help
What is a beacon flood?

An attack that creates hundreds of fake APs by broadcasting forged beacon frames.

Is this real?

No, this is a simulation. No actual wireless frames are transmitted.

What are hidden networks?

APs that don't broadcast their SSID in beacon frames, appearing as blank entries.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.
