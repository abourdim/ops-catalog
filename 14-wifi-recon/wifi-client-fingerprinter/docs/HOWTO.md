# How-To Guide — Client Fingerprinter

## Overview
Simulates passive WiFi client fingerprinting based on behavioral analysis.

## Steps
1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Click **Scan** to begin fingerprinting devices
3. Watch the fingerprint table populate with uniqueness scores
4. Check **Vendor Distribution** for device manufacturer breakdown
5. Click any device row for detailed fingerprint attributes
6. Click **Stop** to halt scanning

## Tips
- Higher uniqueness scores (red) mean the device is more easily trackable
- Fingerprints combine HT capabilities, probe patterns, and timing intervals
- Even with MAC randomization, behavioral fingerprints can identify devices
