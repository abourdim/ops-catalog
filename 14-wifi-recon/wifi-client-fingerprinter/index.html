🎓 How It Works

Client fingerprinting identifies WiFi devices by analyzing their unique behavioral patterns — probe request sequences, supported data rates (HT capabilities), power management flags, and timing intervals. Even with MAC randomization, the combination of these attributes creates a fingerprint that can be used to track and identify devices. The uniqueness score indicates how distinguishable a device is from others in the environment.
