📚 EXIF Field Reference

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) stores metadata in image files. Common fields include camera make/model, exposure settings, GPS coordinates, timestamps, software used, and image dimensions. This data can reveal where and when a photo was taken, the device used, and even software modifications.

🛡️ Privacy Tips

Photos shared online often contain EXIF metadata revealing your location, device, and habits. To protect your privacy: strip EXIF data before sharing, disable GPS tagging in camera settings, use metadata removal tools, and be aware that screenshots also carry device information.

🧪 Forensic Analysis

Digital forensics uses EXIF data to verify image authenticity, track photo origins, and detect tampering. Investigators analyze timestamp consistency, GPS trails, camera serial numbers, and software signatures. Modified images often have mismatched creation/modification dates.

What is Metadata Detective?

A photo forensics simulator that extracts and displays hidden EXIF metadata from images, including GPS coordinates, camera info, and timestamps.

Is this real EXIF data?

No, this app uses simulated EXIF data for educational purposes. No real photos are processed or uploaded.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings and pick your language. Arabic enables RTL automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
