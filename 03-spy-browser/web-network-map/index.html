📚 Network Graph Theory

Network graphs model relationships between entities. Nodes represent agents, edges represent connections. Centrality measures identify key players. Clustering reveals sub-groups.

🔐 Encrypted Communication

In spy networks, messages are encrypted and routed through intermediary nodes. Connection strength indicates frequency of communication. Stronger connections may reveal operational cells.
