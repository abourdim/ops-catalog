# HOWTO — Phishing Trainer

## 1. Read the Scenario
Each question shows an email or URL. Look for red flags.

## 2. Make Your Choice
Click "Legitimate" if you think it is real, or "Phishing" if you think it is fake.

## 3. Learn from Explanations
After answering, read the detailed explanation highlighting red flags or legitimacy indicators.

## 4. Track Your Progress
Your score updates in real-time. Open Section C for detailed statistics.

## 5. Restart
After completing all 12 scenarios, click Restart to play again.
