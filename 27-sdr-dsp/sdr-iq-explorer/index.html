📖 IQ Theory

I/Q (In-phase/Quadrature) representation is fundamental to SDR:

  • Complex signal: s(t) = I(t) + jQ(t)
  • Magnitude: |s| = sqrt(I² + Q²)
  • Phase: φ = atan2(Q, I)
  • IQ imbalance causes image frequency leakage
  • Lissajous pattern reveals phase relationships

❓ Help
What is IQ Explorer?

A tool for visualizing complex I/Q signals with Lissajous patterns and phase analysis.

What is I/Q data?

Two orthogonal signal components (in-phase and quadrature) that represent complex baseband signals.

What is IQ imbalance?

Gain or phase mismatch between I and Q paths, causing image frequency leakage.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally.
