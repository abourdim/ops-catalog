📖 Noise Theory

Understanding noise is critical for SDR receiver design:

  • Thermal noise: P = kTB (Boltzmann constant x Temperature x Bandwidth)
  • Noise Figure: how much noise the receiver adds above thermal
  • MDS: minimum signal level detectable above noise floor
  • Averaging reduces noise by sqrt(N) — 3dB per doubling
  • Dynamic range: span from noise floor to compression point

❓ Help
What is noise floor?

The minimum signal level a receiver can detect, determined by thermal noise and receiver noise figure.

What is noise figure?

How much additional noise the receiver adds above the theoretical thermal noise.

What is MDS?

Minimum Detectable Signal — weakest signal above noise floor, typically with 3dB SNR.
