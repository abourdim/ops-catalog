📖 Signal Recognition Guide

Tips for identifying modulation types by their spectrum:

  • AM: carrier + two symmetric sidebands
  • FM: wider bandwidth, carrier energy spread
  • SSB: single sideband, no carrier
  • BPSK/QPSK: sinc-shaped spectrum, constant envelope
  • CW/Morse: narrow spike, on-off keying pattern on waterfall
