❓ Help
What is RF Waterfall?

A simulated spectrum display that shows radio frequency activity as a scrolling waterfall plot. Colors indicate signal strength.

Are these real signals?

No. This is a simulation for learning purposes. Real signals require an SDR dongle (like RTL-SDR).

How do I tune to a frequency?

Use the frequency tuner sliders or click a preset button (FM, WiFi, ISM) to jump to common bands.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Waterfall Display

A waterfall plot scrolls downward over time. Each horizontal line represents power across the frequency range. Brighter/warmer colors mean stronger signals.

Common Bands

FM Radio: 88-108 MHz, ISM: 433/915 MHz, WiFi 2.4 GHz: 2400-2483 MHz, BLE: 2402-2480 MHz, WiFi 5 GHz: 5150-5850 MHz.
