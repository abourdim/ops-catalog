📡 ISM Bands Explained

ISM (Industrial, Scientific, Medical) bands are unlicensed radio frequency bands available for low-power devices. The 433 MHz band is used in Europe for weather stations, car key fobs, and garage door openers. The 868 MHz band serves LoRa, Sigfox, and other IoT protocols in Europe. The 915 MHz band is the US equivalent. These signals can be received and decoded with an RTL-SDR dongle and rtl_433 software.

❓ Help
What are ISM bands?

Unlicensed radio bands at 433, 868, and 915 MHz used by weather stations, car keys, LoRa, and other IoT devices.

Do I need hardware?

No, this is a simulator. Real scanning uses rtl_433 with an RTL-SDR dongle.

What protocols are shown?

Weather stations, car key fobs, LoRa, garage openers, tire pressure sensors, and more.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.
