❓ Help
What satellites does this receive?

Simulated NOAA 15, 18, and 19 weather satellites at 137 MHz.

What is APT?

Automatic Picture Transmission - analog image format used by NOAA satellites.

Is this real satellite data?

No, this is a simulation. Real reception requires an SDR and V-dipole antenna.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally.
