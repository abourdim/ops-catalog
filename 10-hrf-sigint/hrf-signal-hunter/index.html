What is Signal Hunter?

A game where you find hidden transmitters using signal strength and direction.

How do I find transmitters?

Click on the map to move. The compass and signal strength guide you.

What does hot/cold mean?

Hot = close to a transmitter. Cold = far away. Watch the colors change!

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.
