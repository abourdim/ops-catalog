What is Spectrum Wars?

A game where two players compete for control of frequency bands.

How do I win?

Control more bandwidth than your opponent by placing transmissions and jamming.

What is jamming?

Transmitting noise on an opponent frequency to disrupt their signal.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally.
