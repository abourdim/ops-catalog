📡 GSM/LTE Explained

GSM (Global System for Mobile Communications) and LTE (Long Term Evolution) cell towers create a network of coverage cells. Each tower broadcasts on specific frequency bands and is identified by Cell ID, LAC (Location Area Code), and MCC/MNC (country/network codes). With tools like grgsm_scanner or an RTL-SDR, you can detect nearby towers, measure signal strength, and map their approximate locations. This app simulates that tower scanning experience.

❓ Help
What is this app?

A GSM/LTE cell tower scanner simulator. It shows how SDR tools detect and map nearby cell towers.

Do I need hardware?

No, this is a simulator. Real scanning uses grgsm_scanner with an RTL-SDR dongle.

What is Cell ID?

A unique identifier for each cell tower sector, combined with LAC, MCC, and MNC to identify towers globally.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.
