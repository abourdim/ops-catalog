❓ Help
What is Beacon Trail?

A BLE breadcrumb trail simulator. Drop beacons to mark a path, then follow the trail using signal strength.

How do I drop a beacon?

Click anywhere on the trail map canvas, or press the "Drop Beacon" button for auto-placement.

What is RSSI?

RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator) measures how strong a BLE signal is. Closer beacons have stronger (less negative) RSSI values.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

📡 BLE Beacons

Bluetooth Low Energy beacons are small transmitters that broadcast their identity. They use minimal power and can run for years on a coin cell battery.

📍 iBeacon Protocol

iBeacon is Apple's BLE beacon standard. Each beacon broadcasts a UUID, major value, and minor value to identify its location and context.

🗻 Path Tracking

Path tracking uses ordered beacon sequences as breadcrumbs. By following beacons in order and using RSSI for proximity, you can retrace a route.

🏢 Indoor Navigation

Indoor navigation with BLE beacons enables wayfinding in buildings where GPS is unavailable. Trilateration from multiple beacons estimates position.
