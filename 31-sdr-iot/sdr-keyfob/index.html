📖 Keyfob RF Theory

Car key fobs use simple RF protocols:

  • Common: 315 MHz (US), 433 MHz (EU)
  • ASK/OOK is the simplest modulation
  • Rolling codes prevent replay attacks
  • KeeLoq is widely used for rolling codes
  • Modern cars use encrypted challenge-response
