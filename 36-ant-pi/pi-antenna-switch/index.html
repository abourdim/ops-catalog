📖 Wiring Guide

Connect relay modules to Raspberry Pi GPIO pins. Each relay controls one antenna port through a coaxial switch.

Use GPIO pins 17, 18, 27, 22, 23, 24, 25, and 4 for up to 8 antenna ports. The relay board requires 5V power from the Pi.

For best RF performance, keep coaxial cables short and use proper connectors (N-type or SO-239). Ground all unused ports with 50-ohm terminators.
