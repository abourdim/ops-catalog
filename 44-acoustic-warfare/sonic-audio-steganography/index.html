Can you detect the hidden data by listening to the audio?

No! The encoding uses high-frequency tones near 19-20kHz that are inaudible to most adults. Even with good headphones, the amplitude is too low to perceive.

What is the maximum message size for a 3-second carrier?

With 44100 Hz sample rate and 8 bits per character, a 3-second carrier has 132300 samples, allowing ~16537 characters. In practice, bit duration limits this to around 256 bytes for reliable extraction.

How would a steganalyst detect this encoding?

Spectral analysis would reveal unusual energy peaks at 19-20kHz. Statistical tests (chi-square, RS analysis) can detect non-random patterns in LSB values. Comparing with the original carrier would immediately reveal modifications.

🔒 LSB Encoding

Replace least significant bits of audio samples with message bits. Imperceptible to human ear but detectable by statistical analysis.

📡 Spread Spectrum

Spread message across the frequency spectrum using a pseudo-random sequence. Very robust against compression.

🔊 Echo Hiding

Embed data by introducing micro-echoes. Binary 0/1 mapped to different echo delays (1ms vs 2ms).
