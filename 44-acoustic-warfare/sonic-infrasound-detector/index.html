📚 Infrasound Science
🏆 Challenge

Why can infrasound travel thousands of kilometers while audible sound fades quickly?

Low-frequency waves have very long wavelengths (17m-3400m) which diffract around obstacles and suffer less atmospheric absorption. They can propagate through thermospheric and stratospheric waveguides.

How does the CTBTO distinguish nuclear tests from earthquakes?

Nuclear explosions produce a characteristic frequency pattern (5-20Hz), sharp onset, and specific waveform shape. Triangulation from multiple stations pinpoints the source. Seismic data cross-reference confirms the event type.

What natural phenomenon creates the most powerful infrasound on Earth?

Large volcanic eruptions like Krakatoa (1883) and Hunga Tonga (2022) generated infrasound that circled the globe multiple times, detectable by barometers worldwide at 0.01-5 Hz.

🌋 Infrasound Sources

Earthquakes (0.01-1 Hz), volcanoes (0.5-5 Hz), nuclear tests (0.1-10 Hz), severe weather (1-10 Hz), ocean microbaroms (0.05-0.5 Hz).

📡 Detection Methods

Microbarometers (pressure sensors), infrasound arrays, accelerometers. Arrays use beamforming to determine arrival direction.

🌍 CTBTO Network

60+ stations in the International Monitoring System detect nuclear tests. Each station uses multiple sensors separated by 1-3km for triangulation.
