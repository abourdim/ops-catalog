What is infrasound?

Sound below 20Hz, inaudible to humans but produced by earthquakes, volcanoes, nuclear tests, and severe weather.

Can my mic detect infrasound?

Most MEMS mics have limited low-frequency response. This app amplifies and visualizes what your hardware can capture.
