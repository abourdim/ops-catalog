Why use frequencies above 18kHz for the data link?

Frequencies above 18kHz are inaudible to most adults (presbycusis reduces high-frequency hearing with age). This allows covert data transmission that doesn't disturb people.

Calculate the maximum throughput for 20 bps with 8 data bits per frame.

Each frame is 10 bits (1 start + 8 data + 1 stop). At 20 bps: 20/10 = 2 characters per second = 120 chars/minute. Very slow but sufficient for short messages.

How could an adversary detect and block this data link?

A spectrum analyzer would reveal FSK tones at 18-22kHz. Countermeasures: ultrasonic jammer (noise at same frequencies), bandpass filter, or simply blocking speakers/mics above 17kHz.

📡 FSK Modulation

Frequency-Shift Keying encodes binary data by shifting between two frequencies. Simple, robust, and widely used in modems.

🔊 Ultrasonic Range

18-22kHz range is above most adult hearing. Young people may hear up to 20kHz. Dogs and cats hear up to 40-60kHz.

🔒 Applications

Cross-device pairing, air-gap data exfiltration, proximity verification, indoor positioning, covert communication.
