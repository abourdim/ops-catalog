❓ Help
What is this?

An acoustic modem that transmits text using ultrasonic frequencies above 18kHz — inaudible to most adults.

How does it work?

Each byte is encoded as FSK (Frequency-Shift Keying) tones around the carrier frequency.

Can I send between devices?

Yes — transmit on one device, listen on another nearby device with a microphone.

Is data private?

Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
