What is the Acoustic Fence?

A Doppler-based perimeter detector that emits an ultrasonic tone and detects frequency shifts caused by movement.

How does Doppler detection work?

Moving objects cause reflected sound waves to shift in frequency. Approaching objects increase frequency, retreating objects decrease it.

Range?

Typically 2-5 meters depending on environment and speaker/mic quality.

Privacy?

100% local. No data leaves your browser.
