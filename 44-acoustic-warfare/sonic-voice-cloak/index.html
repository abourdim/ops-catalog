Can pitch shifting alone prevent voice identification?

No! Simple pitch shifting preserves formant patterns, speaking rhythm, and vocabulary. Advanced voice biometrics can reverse pitch shifts. Combine with distortion and formant shifting for better anonymity.

What is the difference between pitch shifting and formant shifting?

Pitch shifting changes the fundamental frequency (how high/low the voice sounds). Formant shifting changes the resonance characteristics of the vocal tract (what makes a voice sound male vs female). Both are needed for convincing disguise.

Design a voice cloak that maximizes anonymity while remaining intelligible.

Use moderate pitch shift (0.7-0.8x), independent formant shift, light distortion, random micro-pauses, and vocabulary substitution. Too much distortion reduces intelligibility. The best cloaks also randomize parameters slightly over time.

What is Voice Cloak?

A real-time voice transformer that changes your voice pitch, timbre, and adds effects to disguise your identity.

Can it truly disguise my identity?

Pitch shifting alone may not fool advanced voice biometrics, but combined with distortion it significantly alters vocal characteristics.

Is there latency?

Web Audio API processes in real-time with minimal latency (typically 10-50ms). Some effects may add slight delay.

Is my audio private?

100% local. Audio is processed entirely in your browser and never leaves your device.

🎵 Pitch Shifting

Changes the fundamental frequency of your voice. Lower values = deeper voice, higher values = chipmunk effect.

🔊 Waveshaping Distortion

Non-linear transfer function that adds harmonics and overtones, creating robotic or demonic effects.

🎛 Biquad Filtering

Shapes frequency response. Low-pass for deep voices, high-pass for thin voices. Controls vocal timbre.
