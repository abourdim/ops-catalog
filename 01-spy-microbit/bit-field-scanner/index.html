🔐 How It Works
1

All five sensors (light, temperature, accelerometer, compass, microphone) are polled simultaneously every scan cycle.

2

Each raw reading is normalized to a 0–100 scale for consistent comparison across different sensor ranges.

3

The threat algorithm combines all normalized readings using configurable weights to produce a single composite score.

4

The composite score determines the alert level: GREEN (below 30), YELLOW (30–70), or RED (above 70).

❓ Help
What is the Field Scanner?

A multi-sensor threat assessment HUD that combines all micro:bit sensors into a single dashboard for environmental monitoring.

How does the threat level work?

Each sensor reading is normalized to 0–100, then combined with weights. The sum determines GREEN (safe), YELLOW (caution), or RED (alert).

Can I customize the threat thresholds?

Yes! Open the Lab section and use the rules editor to define custom threat conditions and adjust sensor weights.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
