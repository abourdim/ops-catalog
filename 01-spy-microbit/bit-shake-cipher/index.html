What does this app do?

It's like a spy gadget simulator! 🕵️ You get to play with real encryption, secret messages, and covert communication — the same tech real spies use.

How does it work?

The simulation runs right in your browser. It shows you step by step how secret agents protect their messages using math and radio signals.

Hit the main button and watch what happens! 🎯 Then try changing the settings to see how it affects the results.

What's the real science?

This uses real cryptography — the same math that protects your WhatsApp messages and bank passwords! 🔐

100% safe! 🛡️ Everything runs locally in your browser. No internet needed, no data leaves your device.

📊 Accelerometer

A sensor that measures proper acceleration (g-force) along 3 axes. The micro:bit accelerometer range is +/- 2g with 10-bit resolution.

🔐 Pattern Recognition

Algorithms that identify regularities in data. Here we use Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) to match shake patterns despite timing variations.

🛡️ Physical Authentication

Using physical actions (gestures, shakes, movements) as passwords. More intuitive than text passwords but requires pattern matching algorithms.

📡 micro:bit Sensors

The micro:bit v2 includes: accelerometer, magnetometer, temperature sensor, microphone, touch sensor, and light level sensor.
