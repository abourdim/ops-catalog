🧲 Magnetometer

A magnetometer measures the strength and direction of magnetic fields. In navigation, it detects Earth's field to determine compass heading.

🧭 Compass Bearing

A bearing is an angle measured clockwise from North (0°) to the direction of a target. East = 90°, South = 180°, West = 270°.

📍 Waypoint Navigation

Waypoint navigation involves moving from point to point using bearings. Each waypoint has coordinates or a bearing from a reference point.

💻 micro:bit Compass

The micro:bit v2 includes an LSM303AGR magnetometer. After calibration, it provides heading data for compass applications.
