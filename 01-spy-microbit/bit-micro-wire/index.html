❓ Help
How does the microphone work on micro:bit?

The micro:bit V2 has a built-in MEMS microphone that converts sound waves into electrical signals. This app simulates that process using the Web Audio API.

What is audio sampling?

Audio sampling captures sound at regular intervals. At 8 kHz / 8-bit, we take 8,000 measurements per second, each stored as a value from 0 to 255.

What is BLE bandwidth for audio?

BLE 4.2 supports ~20-byte payloads per packet. At 8 kHz/8-bit (8 KB/s), you need about 400 packets/second — tight but feasible with connection interval tuning.

Is my audio data private?

Yes. All audio processing is local in your browser. No audio data is sent to any server. The transcription is simulated with random phrases.

🎵 Audio Sampling

Sound is digitized by measuring amplitude at fixed intervals. The Nyquist theorem requires sampling at 2x the highest frequency. At 8 kHz we capture frequencies up to 4 kHz — enough for voice.

📡 BLE Data Channels

BLE uses 37 data channels (2.4 GHz). Each packet carries up to 20 bytes (BLE 4.0/4.1) or 244 bytes (BLE 5.0+). Connection intervals of 7.5 ms to 4 s control throughput.

📝 Voice Transcription

Real transcription uses speech-to-text models (like Whisper or Google STT). This app simulates it with random phrases to demonstrate the concept without cloud services.

🔌 micro:bit Microphone

The micro:bit V2 MEMS microphone detects sound levels and patterns. MakeCode and MicroPython expose it via simple APIs for sound-triggered events and level readings.
