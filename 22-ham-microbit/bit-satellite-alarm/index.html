What does this app do?

It simulates ham radio! 🔬 You get to experiment with radio signals in a safe sandbox.

How does it work?

The simulation runs in your browser. It models real radio signals so you can see what happens step by step.

What should I try first?

Press the main button and watch! 🎯 Then tweak the settings to see how different parameters change the results.

What's the real science?

This is real amateur radio communication! The same principles are used by professionals in the field. 🧪

Can I break it?

Try the Lab section! Push the parameters to extremes and see what happens. That's how scientists discover new things! 💡

What hardware do I need?

For the real version, you'll need BBC micro:bit V2. Check the 📦 Device Code section for ready-to-use firmware!

Is it safe to use?

Absolutely safe! 🛡️ Everything runs locally in your browser. No internet required, no data leaves your device.

🌍 Orbital Mechanics

Satellites orbit Earth following Kepler's laws. The ISS orbits at ~408 km altitude with a 51.6° inclination, completing one orbit every ~92 minutes at 7.66 km/s.

🔮 Pass Prediction

A pass occurs when a satellite rises above the observer's horizon. Predictions use the satellite's orbital elements (TLE) and the observer's coordinates to compute rise/set times.

📐 Elevation & Azimuth

Elevation measures the angle from the horizon (0°) to zenith (90°). Azimuth measures the compass bearing from North (0°) clockwise. Together they pinpoint any sky position.

🛰️ ISS Facts

The International Space Station is the largest human-made object in space. It orbits at ~28,000 km/h and is visible to the naked eye, appearing as a bright moving star at dawn or dusk.
