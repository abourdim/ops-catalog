📖BLE Frequency Hopping Theory

BLE uses 40 channels in the 2.4 GHz ISM band (2402-2480 MHz), each 2 MHz wide. 37 are data channels, 3 are advertising (37, 38, 39).

Connected devices hop across data channels at 1600 hops/second using an algorithm seeded by the access address, making the pattern unique per connection.

Adaptive Frequency Hopping (AFH) lets BLE skip channels with interference (e.g., from WiFi), improving coexistence in the crowded 2.4 GHz band.

The waterfall display shows channel activity over time. Bright dots indicate active transmissions on that channel at that moment.
