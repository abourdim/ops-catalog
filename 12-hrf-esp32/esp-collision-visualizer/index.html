📖Collision Theory

When two transmitters broadcast on the same frequency simultaneously, their signals overlap and interfere, causing bit errors in the received data.

The Bit Error Rate (BER) depends on the relative power of the two signals. Equal power causes maximum interference (capture effect threshold).

CSMA/CA (WiFi) and frequency hopping (BLE) are designed to minimize collisions, but cannot eliminate them in the crowded 2.4 GHz band.

The simulation shows how power difference between TX1 and TX2 affects BER — stronger signals dominate weaker ones (capture effect).
