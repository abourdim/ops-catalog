📚 Game Rules
1️⃣ Teams

Two teams: Red and Blue. Each player joins one team. Teams work together to find and capture flags.

2️⃣ Clues

ESP32 beacons are hidden around the play area. Each beacon broadcasts encrypted clues via BLE. Use the scanner to receive and decode them.

3️⃣ Flags

Each clue leads to a flag code (format: FLAG{XXXX}). Enter the code to capture the flag. Each flag is worth points. First team to submit a flag gets bonus points.

4️⃣ Winning

Game lasts 5 minutes. The team with the most points when time runs out wins. Flags: 100 pts. First capture bonus: 50 pts. Speed bonus: up to 25 pts.

❓ Help
What is Capture The Flag?

A physical team game where ESP32 beacons broadcast clues. Teams scan, decode, and submit flag codes to score points.

Do I need real ESP32s?

No. The app simulates ESP32 broadcasts with fake clues and flags. Perfect for learning the concept without hardware.

How do I score points?

Capture flags (100 pts), be first to capture (50 bonus), and submit quickly for speed bonus (up to 25 pts).

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🚩 CTF

Capture The Flag — a competition where teams find and capture hidden tokens (flags) for points.

📶 BLE Beacons

Bluetooth Low Energy beacons broadcast small data packets. ESP32s can act as BLE beacons to transmit clues.

🔐 Ciphers

Simple encryption methods used to encode clues. Teams must decode them to find the flag.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first, privacy-first. All data stays in your browser.
