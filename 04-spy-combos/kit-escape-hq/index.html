📚 How To Escape
1️⃣ Start the Timer

Click "Start Escape" to begin. You have 10 minutes to solve all 4 puzzles and unlock the exit door. Each puzzle unlocks one of four locks.

2️⃣ Solve Puzzles

Click a puzzle card to open it. Cipher Lock: decode a Caesar cipher. Pattern Grid: memorize and recreate. Sequence Key: find the missing number. Logic Gate: answer true/false.

3️⃣ Collect Items

Solving puzzles adds items to your inventory. Some items are clues for other puzzles. Check your inventory regularly for hints.

4️⃣ Escape!

When all 4 locks are unlocked, the exit door opens. Your escape time is recorded. Can you beat the record?

🧩 Puzzle Encyclopedia
🔤 Caesar Cipher

One of the oldest encryption methods. Each letter is shifted by a fixed number. A shift of 3 turns A into D, B into E, etc. Named after Julius Caesar who used it for military messages.

🔲 Pattern Recognition

Visual memory challenge. A pattern flashes on a grid, and you must recreate it from memory. Trains spatial awareness and short-term visual recall.

🔢 Number Sequences

Mathematical patterns where each number follows a rule. Finding the rule and predicting the next number trains logical thinking and pattern analysis.

🧠 Logic Gates

AND, OR, NOT, XOR — the building blocks of digital circuits and computer processors. Understanding logic gates is fundamental to electronics and programming.

🏆 Speed Challenges
⚡ Speed Escape

Complete all 4 puzzles in under 3 minutes. Puzzles are randomized each attempt.

🔥 Hardcore Mode

Puzzles get progressively harder. Cipher shift increases, pattern grid gets larger, sequences use harder rules.
