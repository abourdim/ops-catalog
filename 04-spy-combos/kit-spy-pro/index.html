📚 Device Setup Guide
1️⃣ Wearable (micro:bit)

The micro:bit acts as your wearable agent device. It monitors heart rate, step count, and sends BLE alerts. Wear it on your wrist during missions for real-time biometric telemetry.

2️⃣ Field Device (ESP32)

The ESP32 is your field scanner. It detects WiFi signals, measures temperature, and relays encrypted data back to HQ via MQTT. Deploy it at observation points.

3️⃣ Browser HQ

This browser app is your headquarters. It receives telemetry from both devices, displays the agent tracker map, manages missions, and handles encrypted communications.

4️⃣ Network Architecture

Wearable connects via BLE to field device. Field device connects via WiFi/MQTT to HQ. All messages are encrypted with AES-256. The system forms a secure mesh.

🗺️ Mission Briefings
🔍 Recon Mission

Observe the target area. Deploy ESP32 sensors at 3 checkpoints. Collect signal strength data from each point. Report findings via encrypted channel.

📡 Intercept Mission

A rogue signal has been detected. Use the field device to triangulate the source. The wearable will alert you when you are within range. Capture and decode the transmission.

🚁 Extraction Mission

Agent is at an unknown location. Use the HQ map to track their wearable signal. Guide them to the extraction point using encrypted comms. Time is critical.

❓ Help
What is Spy Kit Pro?

A 3-device spy system simulator: wearable (micro:bit), field scanner (ESP32), and browser HQ. Learn IoT, encrypted comms, and mission coordination.

Do I need real hardware?

No. Everything is simulated. Fake telemetry is generated to demonstrate how the real system would work.

What is the encrypted channel?

Messages are simulated with AES-256 encryption. You type plaintext, and the system shows the encrypted form and decoded result.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

📶 BLE

Bluetooth Low Energy — short-range wireless used by the micro:bit wearable to transmit biometric data.

📡 MQTT

Message Queuing Telemetry Transport — lightweight IoT protocol used by the ESP32 to relay data to HQ.

🔐 AES-256

Advanced Encryption Standard with 256-bit keys — the gold standard for secure communications.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first, privacy-first. All data stays in your browser. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls.
