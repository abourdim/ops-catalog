        🔧 How It Works
        

          

            
1
The ISS orbits Earth every ~92 minutes at 408 km altitude, traveling at 7.66 km/s.

            
2
Amateur radio on ISS uses 145.800 MHz FM downlink for voice and packet.

            
3
Doppler shift changes the frequency by up to +/-3.5 kHz during a pass.

            
4
A pass lasts 5-10 minutes — the higher the max elevation, the longer and stronger.

Pro Tips
Did You Know?
Common Mistakes

        
🛸 International Space Station
The ISS orbits at ~408 km altitude with amateur radio equipment and automatic APRS digipeater.

        
📡 145.800 MHz
Worldwide ISS downlink frequency for FM voice and APRS. Uplink is typically 145.990 MHz.

        
🔄 Doppler Effect
Relative motion between ISS and ground station causes frequency shift that must be compensated.

        
📦 APRS on ISS
The ISS carries a packet radio digipeater. Ground stations send APRS messages via ISS on 145.825 MHz.
