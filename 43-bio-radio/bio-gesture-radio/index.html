❓ Help
How are gestures detected?

Flex sensors on each finger change resistance when bent. The micro:bit ADC reads 5 analog channels, one per finger.

What is BLE advertising?

Bluetooth Low Energy advertising broadcasts small data packets without pairing. Perfect for one-way gesture transmission.

Can this be intercepted?

BLE has short range (~10m). With encryption, even nearby listeners cannot decode the gesture stream.

How many gestures are possible?

With 5 binary finger states, 32 combinations exist. In practice, 6-10 distinct gestures are reliably recognized.

✋ Gesture Recognition

5 flex sensors create a binary finger map. Each gesture is a unique 5-bit pattern matched against a library.

📡 BLE Advertising

BLE advertising packets carry up to 31 bytes of data. Gesture codes fit in 2-3 bytes with room for sequence numbers and checksums.

🔒 Encryption

XOR cipher with a pre-shared key. Each gesture code is encrypted before BLE transmission, making interception useless.
