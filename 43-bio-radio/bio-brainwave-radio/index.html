📚 A — How It Works
🧠1

EEG Pickup

Electrodes on the scalp detect tiny electrical signals from brain neuron activity (10-100 microvolts).

📊2

FFT Analysis

Fast Fourier Transform decomposes signals into frequency bands: Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta waves.

📡3

RF Modulation

Brain state data modulates a radio carrier. Focus level controls the modulation index.

📶4

Transmission

micro:bit transmits the modulated signal. Another micro:bit receives and decodes the brain state.

❓ Help
What are brainwaves?

Electrical signals produced by neurons. Different frequencies indicate different mental states.

Can I really transmit thoughts?

Not individual thoughts, but brain states (relaxed, focused) can be detected and transmitted via radio.

What does each band mean?

Delta: deep sleep. Theta: drowsiness. Alpha: relaxed. Beta: alert/focused. Gamma: high cognition.

🧠 EEG Bands
