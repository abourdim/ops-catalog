📚 A — How It Works
🌡1

IR + RF Sensing

Humans emit ~10µm infrared. WiFi/radar signals reflect differently off warm bodies.

🧱2

Wall Penetration

WiFi (2.4GHz) penetrates drywall/wood. Signal attenuation reveals body positions.

📊3

Signal Processing

Doppler shifts and signal strength changes mapped to thermal signatures.

🗺4

Heatmap Generation

Processed data rendered as thermal-style heatmap showing human positions.
