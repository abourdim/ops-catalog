❓ Help
What is GSR?

Galvanic Skin Response measures electrical conductance of the skin, which changes with sweat gland activity driven by emotions.

Why is GSR good for keys?

The least significant bits of GSR readings contain high-entropy noise from biological processes that is nearly impossible to predict.

Is this truly random?

GSR provides biological randomness. Combined with proper conditioning, it can seed cryptographic random number generators.
