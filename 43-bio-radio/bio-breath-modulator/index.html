📚 A — How It Works
🫁1

Breath Sensor

Thermistor near nostrils detects airflow temperature changes during breathing cycles.

📈2

Envelope Extraction

ADC captures breathing waveform. Low-pass filter extracts the slow breathing envelope.

📡3

FM Modulation

Breathing envelope modulates 433.92 MHz carrier via frequency deviation (FM).

📶4

Demodulation

Receiver demodulates FM signal to reconstruct the breathing pattern remotely.
