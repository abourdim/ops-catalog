📚 A — How It Works
🧍1

Body as Antenna

The human body acts as a ~1.7m antenna, resonant near 1.8 MHz. micro:bit ADC measures the RF signal picked up.

📏2

Impedance Probe

A simple bridge circuit measures body impedance — the complex resistance to RF signals at different frequencies.

📈3

SWR Calculation

Standing Wave Ratio shows how well the body antenna is matched. SWR = 1.0 is perfect, higher means mismatch.

🔊4

Signal Detection

Touch detection changes the antenna pattern. Grounding improves reception. Nearby AM stations become audible!

🏆 C — Challenges
1

Find the Resonance

2

Ground Effect

Touch a grounded metal surface while scanning. What happens to the impedance reading?

3

AM Radio Pickup

Sweep 500 kHz to 1700 kHz. Can you detect any AM radio stations using your body as antenna?

📡 Body Antenna Theory

A 1.7m human body resonates at ~1.8 MHz (quarter-wave). Grounding one end creates a monopole antenna with ~36 ohm impedance.

⚡ Impedance

Z = R + jX. Real part R is resistance, imaginary part jX is reactance. Measured in ohms. SWR indicates matching quality.
