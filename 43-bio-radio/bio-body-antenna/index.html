❓ Help
Can my body really be an antenna?

Yes! Any conductive object can receive RF. The human body, being ~1.7m tall and conductive, resonates near 1.8 MHz.

What is impedance?

Impedance (Z) is the total opposition to current flow in an AC circuit. It has real (resistance) and imaginary (reactance) parts.

Is this safe?

Absolutely. We are only measuring signals already passing through your body. No energy is transmitted into you.

📡 Body Antenna Theory

A 1.7m human body resonates at ~1.8 MHz (quarter-wave). Grounding one end creates a monopole antenna with ~36 ohm impedance.

⚡ Impedance

Z = R + jX. Real part R is resistance, imaginary part jX is reactance. Measured in ohms. SWR indicates matching quality.
