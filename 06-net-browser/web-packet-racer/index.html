📚 Network Obstacles

Firewalls (red) block packets — find alternate routes. Congestion zones (orange) slow your speed. Packet loss areas (purple) reduce your health. Routers (green) are safe checkpoints that give bonus points.

🛡️ Network Concepts

Packets travel through hops (routers). Each hop adds latency. Firewalls filter traffic by rules. Congestion occurs when bandwidth is exceeded. TTL (Time To Live) prevents infinite loops.
