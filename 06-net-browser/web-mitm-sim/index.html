📚 How MITM Attacks Work

In a Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) attack, Eve secretly relays and possibly alters communications between Alice and Bob. Neither party knows Eve is intercepting their messages. Without encryption, Eve can read everything in plaintext. With TLS/SSL encryption, Eve sees only scrambled ciphertext and cannot read the actual content.

🛡️ Famous MITM Scenarios

Public Wi-Fi hotspots are a common MITM attack vector. ARP spoofing redirects traffic through the attacker. DNS spoofing sends victims to fake websites. SSL stripping downgrades HTTPS to HTTP. Always check for the padlock icon and verify certificates to stay safe.

🛡️ Defense Strategies

Learn how to defend against MITM attacks with HTTPS, certificate pinning, HSTS, and mutual TLS authentication.
