📖 Jupiter Radio Science

Jupiter is the strongest radio source in the solar system after the Sun:

  • Decametric emissions occur between 10-40 MHz
  • Io-induced storms are triggered by the Io flux tube
  • S-bursts are short (<1ms) with frequency drift ~20 MHz/s
  • L-bursts last 0.5-5 seconds with broadband character
  • Best received with a dipole antenna at ~20 MHz

❓ Help
What is the Jupiter Receiver?

A simulator for receiving Jupiter's decametric radio emissions including Io-induced storms.

What frequency range?

Jupiter emits strongly between 18-28 MHz in the decametric band.

What are S-bursts and L-bursts?

S-bursts are short millisecond pulses; L-bursts are longer broadband emissions lasting seconds.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.
