📖 RFI Theory

RF Interference fundamentals:

  • Narrowband spurs: single-frequency interference from oscillators
  • Broadband noise: switching power supplies, motors, LED drivers
  • Harmonics: integer multiples of a fundamental frequency
  • Pulsed interference: radar, industrial equipment
  • Spectrograms show time vs frequency to reveal intermittent RFI

What is the Interference Finder?

A tool to detect and visualize RF interference using spectrogram analysis.

What types of interference?

Narrowband spurs, broadband noise, pulsed interference and harmonics.

How does detection work?

The spectrogram highlights power anomalies above the noise floor.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
