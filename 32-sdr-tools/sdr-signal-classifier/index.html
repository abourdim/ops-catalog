📖 Modulation Theory

Signal modulation fundamentals:

  • AM: amplitude varies, carrier visible in spectrum
  • FM: constant envelope, frequency deviation carries information
  • PSK: phase changes at symbol boundaries (BPSK, QPSK)
  • QAM: both amplitude and phase vary (16-QAM, 64-QAM)
  • OFDM: multiple subcarriers, used in Wi-Fi and LTE

What is the Signal Classifier?

An AI-assisted tool that identifies modulation types from signal features.

What modulations are detected?

AM, FM, SSB, PSK, QAM, FSK, OFDM and CW.

How does classification work?

Feature extraction analyzes bandwidth, symmetry, and cyclostationary properties.
