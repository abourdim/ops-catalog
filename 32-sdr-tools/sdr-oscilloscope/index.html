📖 Oscilloscope Theory

Oscilloscope fundamentals:

  • Timebase (ms/div) controls horizontal scale
  • Trigger stabilizes repeating waveforms on screen
  • Vpp = peak-to-peak voltage, Vrms = root mean square
  • For sine waves: Vrms = Vpp / (2 * sqrt(2))
  • Bandwidth = highest frequency the scope can display

❓ Help
What is the SDR Oscilloscope?

A time-domain signal viewer that displays waveforms like a real oscilloscope.

What waveforms are available?

Sine, square, triangle, sawtooth, noise and AM/FM modulated signals.

Can I adjust timebase?

Yes, use the ms/div control to change the horizontal scale.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.
