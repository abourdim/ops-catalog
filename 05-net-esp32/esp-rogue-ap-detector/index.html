🏆 Challenge

How does an evil twin attack work?

An attacker creates an AP with the same SSID as a legitimate one. Victims connect to the fake AP, allowing the attacker to intercept all traffic.

Why can't you rely on SSID alone?

SSIDs are just names — anyone can create an AP with any name. The BSSID (MAC) is harder to spoof but still possible. Multi-factor fingerprinting is needed.

What additional checks could detect a rogue AP?

Check beacon interval timing, probe response patterns, supported rates, vendor OUI of the BSSID, certificate validation for WPA-Enterprise, and physical signal direction.

❓ Help
What is an evil twin?

A rogue AP that copies a legitimate network's SSID to trick users into connecting.

What is BSSID?

The MAC address of the access point's radio. Each AP has a unique BSSID.

Can evil twins be detected?

Yes, by comparing fingerprints: BSSID, channel, encryption type, beacon intervals, and signal patterns.

Is this a real scanner?
