🏆 Challenge

Why do BLE devices appear and disappear frequently?

BLE devices use advertising intervals. They broadcast periodically and sleep between ads to save battery. If you miss the ad window, the device seems gone.

How does RSSI relate to distance?

RSSI decreases with distance following the inverse square law. -30dBm is very close, -70dBm is medium, -90dBm is far. Walls and obstacles cause additional loss.

What is ESP-NOW and how is it different from WiFi?

ESP-NOW is a connectionless protocol by Espressif. It sends small packets directly between ESP32 devices without needing a router or AP. Lower latency than WiFi, max 250 bytes per packet.

❓ Help
What protocols does it scan?

WiFi (802.11), Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), and ESP-NOW — all in the 2.4GHz band.

What does the map show?

Each circle is a detected device. Size and position reflect signal strength. Color indicates protocol type.

Is this scanning real devices?

No. This is a simulation with fake devices for educational purposes.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.

📶 WiFi (802.11)

Wireless LAN protocol. Access points broadcast beacons. Devices connect for internet access. Range: 30-100m.

📱 BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy)

Short-range protocol for IoT devices, fitness trackers, beacons. Advertising-based discovery. Range: 10-50m.

📡 ESP-NOW

Espressif connectionless protocol for ESP32-to-ESP32 communication. No router needed. 250 byte max payload.

📊 RSSI

Received Signal Strength Indicator in dBm. -30 = excellent, -50 = good, -70 = fair, -90 = weak.
