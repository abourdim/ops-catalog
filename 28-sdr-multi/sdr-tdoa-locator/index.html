📊 TDOA Results

TDOA measures the time difference a signal takes to reach multiple receivers. The intersection of hyperbolas defined by time differences gives the transmitter location.

📖 Theory & Notes
  • TDOA uses time differences, not absolute times — no clock sync needed at TX
  • Each receiver pair defines a hyperbola of possible TX locations
  • 3+ receivers needed for 2D location, 4+ for 3D
  • Precision depends on receiver geometry and synchronization accuracy
