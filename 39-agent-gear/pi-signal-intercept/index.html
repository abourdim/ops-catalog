What is signal interception?

Signal interception is the capture and analysis of radio transmissions across frequency bands for intelligence gathering.

What frequency bands can I scan?

VHF (30-300 MHz), UHF (300-3000 MHz), and SHF (3-30 GHz) bands with AM, FM, SSB, and digital modulation.

How does the waterfall display work?

The waterfall shows signal intensity over time and frequency. Bright colors indicate strong signals.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere. This is a simulation only.

📡 SIGINT

Signals Intelligence (SIGINT) involves intercepting electronic signals for reconnaissance and analysis.

📻 SDR

Software Defined Radio (SDR) uses software to process radio signals, replacing traditional hardware components.

🔊 Modulation

Modulation encodes data onto carrier waves using AM, FM, SSB, or digital techniques like PSK and QAM.
