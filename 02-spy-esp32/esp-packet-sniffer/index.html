📡 Monitor Mode

Promiscuous/monitor mode lets the WiFi chip capture ALL frames, not just those addressed to it. The ESP32 supports this natively.

📦 802.11 Frames

Three main types: Management (beacons, probes, auth), Control (RTS/CTS, ACK), and Data (actual payload). Each has a 2-byte frame control field.

📶 RSSI

Received Signal Strength Indicator measures signal power in dBm. Closer to 0 = stronger. Typical: -30 (excellent) to -90 (weak).

🔒 Privacy

Local-first, privacy-first. All data stays in your browser. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls.
