What is Frequency Hunter?

An educational RF scanning simulator. Learn about radio frequencies, signal strength, and direction finding by hunting hidden transmitters.

How does the hunt work?

A hidden transmitter is placed at a random frequency. Use the tuner slider to scan and follow the hot/cold RSSI feedback to locate it.

What is RSSI?

RSSI stands for Received Signal Strength Indicator. It measures how strong a radio signal is. Higher values mean you are closer to the source.

Is this a real scanner?

No, this is a simulation for learning purposes. All signals are generated locally in your browser. No real radio hardware is used.

1

📻 RF Scanning

Radio frequency scanning involves sweeping through a range of frequencies to detect active transmissions. Real scanners use antennas and receivers to pick up signals.

📶 Signal Strength

RSSI is measured in dBm (decibels relative to milliwatt). Typical values: -90 dBm (very weak), -60 dBm (moderate), -30 dBm (strong), 0 dBm (maximum).

🧭 Direction Finding

Direction finding uses signal strength from different angles to triangulate a transmitter location. Moving and comparing RSSI readings helps pinpoint the source.

📡 Frequency Bands

Common bands: ISM 433 MHz (remote controls), ISM 868/915 MHz (LoRa, IoT), WiFi 2.4 GHz, FM Radio 88-108 MHz. Each band has different propagation characteristics.
