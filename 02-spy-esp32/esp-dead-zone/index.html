❓ Help
What is Dead Zone?

Dead Zone is an educational WiFi device detector simulator. It teaches you how ESP32-based scanners discover nearby wireless devices by capturing probe requests and beacon frames.

What are probe requests?

Probe requests are WiFi frames that devices send to discover known networks. They reveal previously connected SSIDs, exposing your travel and location history.

Can this detect hidden cameras?

Yes! Hidden WiFi cameras emit detectable wireless signals. This tool identifies suspicious devices by analyzing MAC vendor prefixes, signal patterns, and probe behavior.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere. This is a simulation for learning purposes.

What is MAC address randomization?

Modern devices use random MAC addresses when scanning for WiFi to prevent tracking. The local bit (second hex character is 2,6,A,E) indicates a randomized address.

📡 Probe Requests

WiFi devices constantly send probe requests to find known networks. Each probe contains the device MAC and often the SSID it is looking for, creating a wireless fingerprint.

🏷️ MAC Addresses

The first 3 bytes (OUI) of a MAC address identify the manufacturer. This allows vendor identification even without connecting to the device.

🔬 Device Fingerprinting

Beyond MAC addresses, devices can be identified by probe timing patterns, supported rates, HT capabilities, and other 802.11 frame fields.

🕵️ Surveillance Detection

Hidden cameras and tracking devices emit WiFi signals that can be detected. Look for always-on devices with strong signals from surveillance equipment manufacturers.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first, privacy-first. All data stays in your browser. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls.
