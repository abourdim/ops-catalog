🏆 Challenge

What makes a pretext convincing?

Specific details about the target, urgency preventing verification, authority figures discouraging questioning, and emotional triggers.

How can organizations defend against pretexting?

Verification procedures, employee training, callback protocols, and a culture encouraging questioning of unusual requests.

Design a pretext detection checklist.

Check: unusual urgency, caller verifiability, procedure bypasses, emotional pressure, credential requests, independent verification possibility.
