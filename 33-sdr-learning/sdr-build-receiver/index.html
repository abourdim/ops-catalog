📖 Receiver Theory

A superheterodyne receiver converts RF signals to baseband audio through these stages:

  • Antenna captures electromagnetic waves as voltage signals
  • LNA amplifies weak signals while adding minimal noise
  • Mixer multiplies RF with local oscillator for frequency conversion
  • IF filter selects desired signal bandwidth
  • Demodulator extracts baseband audio from carrier
  • AGC maintains consistent output level
