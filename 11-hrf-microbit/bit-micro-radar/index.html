❓ Help
What is this app?

A micro:bit radar simulator that teaches electromagnetic detection, polar coordinates, and sensor-based mapping.

How does the radar work?

The sweep line rotates 360 degrees. Objects are detected as blips when the line passes their angular position. Blips fade over time like a real radar.

What is the difference between Active and Passive mode?

Active mode detects all objects by sending out signals. Passive mode only detects objects that emit their own electromagnetic signals.

Can I place my own objects?

Yes! Click anywhere on the radar canvas to place a virtual object. It will appear on the next sweep pass.

📡 Radar Principles

Radar (Radio Detection and Ranging) works by sending out electromagnetic waves and measuring reflections. The time delay and angle reveal object position and distance.

🎯 Polar Coordinates

Unlike Cartesian (x,y) grids, polar coordinates use angle and distance from a center point. This is natural for radar displays where the antenna is at the origin.

⚡ EM Detection

Electromagnetic detection includes both active (sending signals and listening for echoes) and passive (listening for signals emitted by objects) methods.

🤖 micro:bit Sensors

The micro:bit has an accelerometer (motion/tilt) and magnetometer (compass/direction). These can simulate radar antenna orientation and sweep control.
