🏆 Challenge

Why do phones automatically connect to evil twin APs?

Phones remember previously connected SSIDs and auto-join when they see matching names. Evil twins broadcast the same SSID with stronger signal, and most devices do not verify the AP's BSSID or certificate.

How does a KARMA attack work differently from an evil twin?

KARMA responds to ALL probe requests from nearby devices, pretending to be whatever SSID each device is looking for. Instead of targeting one network, it impersonates every remembered network simultaneously.

Design a defense against evil twin attacks for a corporate network.

Deploy WPA3-Enterprise with certificate-based authentication (EAP-TLS). Use WIDS to detect duplicate SSIDs with different BSSIDs. Configure clients to verify server certificates. Deploy management frame protection (802.11w) to prevent deauth attacks.
