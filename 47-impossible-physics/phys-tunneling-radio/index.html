📖 Theory

Quantum tunneling allows particles to pass through classically forbidden barriers:

  • Wave function doesn't vanish inside the barrier — it decays exponentially
  • Transmission probability depends on barrier width and height vs particle energy
  • The Hartman effect: tunneling time can appear instantaneous
  • Resonant tunneling through double barriers creates transmission peaks
  • No classical analog — purely quantum mechanical phenomenon
