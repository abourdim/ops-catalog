📖 Theory

Superluminal illusions arise without violating relativity:

  • Group velocity can exceed c in anomalous dispersion media
  • No information travels faster than light — signal velocity is always ≤ c
  • Phase velocity exceeds c in many ordinary materials
  • Light scissors effect: apparent velocity at intersection point is unlimited
  • Shadows and spots can sweep across surfaces faster than c
