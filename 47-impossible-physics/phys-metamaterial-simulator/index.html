📖 Metamaterial Theory

      
Metamaterials are engineered structures with unusual EM properties:

      
  • Negative refractive index bends light the wrong way
  • Split-ring resonators create artificial magnetic response
  • Transformation optics enables electromagnetic cloaking
  • Perfect lensing can beat the diffraction limit
  • Left-handed materials reverse the Poynting vector

🔄 Negative Refraction
When both permittivity and permeability are negative, Snell's law reverses.

        
👻 EM Cloaking
Transformation optics maps coordinates to material parameters, guiding waves around hidden regions.

        
💍 Split-Ring Resonators
Concentric metallic rings with gaps that resonate magnetically at microwave frequencies.
