📖 Theory

Plasma antennas use ionized gas instead of metal for RF radiation:

  • Ionized gas columns act as conductive antenna elements
  • Plasma density controls the antenna's operating frequency
  • When de-ionized, the antenna becomes invisible to radar
  • Reconfigurable patterns by controlling ionization profiles
  • Near-zero thermal noise when plasma is quenched
