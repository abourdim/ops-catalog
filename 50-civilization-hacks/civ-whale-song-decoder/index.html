What does this app do?

It simulates civilization monitoring! 🔬 You get to experiment with environmental sensors in a safe sandbox.

The simulation runs in your browser. It models real environmental sensors so you can see what happens step by step.

What should I try first?

Press the main button and watch! 🎯 Then tweak the settings to see how different parameters change the results.

This is real disaster detection and response! The same principles are used by professionals in the field. 🧪

📡 BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy)

A wireless protocol designed for low power. Devices broadcast advertising packets that nearby scanners can detect. Range: 10-50m.

🔑 XOR Cipher

A symmetric cipher: plaintext XOR key = ciphertext. Ciphertext XOR key = plaintext. Simple but effective for learning.

📨 Dead Drop Protocol

Agent A encrypts and broadcasts. Agent B scans and decrypts. No direct connection needed — just shared airwaves and a shared key.

📻 micro:bit Radio

micro:bit v2 uses a 2.4GHz radio for BLE and custom radio groups. Packets are limited to ~20 bytes. RSSI measures signal strength.
